The exact rules StockArtha scans for

Same logic ships as a Pine Script v5 file — paste it into TradingView for free backtesting before you deploy capital.

1 · Strong wick rejection

lower wick ≥ 2 × body, close in top 30% of range, bullish, volume > 20 SMA.

2 · Bullish order block

Last bearish candle before an impulse of ≥ 2 × ATR(14) within 3 bars. Its high/low form the zone.

3 · Inducement not mitigated

Price returns, sweeps the OB low by ≤ 0.3 × ATR and closes back above. Already-mitigated blocks are ignored.

4 · 4H RSI < 50

Higher-timeframe RSI(14) on 4-hour candles must be under 50 at the trigger bar.

5 · Trade management

Entry at trigger close, stop 3%, target 10%, time exit after 25 bars.

Questions traders ask

Do I need a paid data API or broker account?

No. Live quotes, daily candles and hourly candles come from free public endpoints (Yahoo Finance, NSE/BSE archives). Dhan is optional and only if you want live order execution.

Can I backtest before risking money?

Yes — two ways. The bundled Pine Script runs in TradingView's free Strategy Tester, and StockArtha itself shows per-symbol backtest stats (win rate, profit factor, drawdown) plus a paper portfolio on real prices.

Is this investment advice?

No. StockArtha is analysis and automation tooling. ICT concepts (order blocks, inducement) have no universal definition, so the exact rules are printed on every chart and in the docs. Trade at your own risk.

How do I get alerts on my phone?

In-app notifications, a free Telegram bot, and a TradingView webhook endpoint. Settings → Alerts gives you the webhook URL and passphrase.

Is my account private?

Yes. Accounts are stored locally in the app's own database with scrypt-hashed passwords. Nothing is sold or shared. The owner account is private and has no public access.
