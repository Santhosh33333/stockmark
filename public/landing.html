Do I need a paid data API or broker account?

No. Live quotes and candles come from free public endpoints (Yahoo Finance, NSE/BSE archives). Dhan is optional and only for live order execution.

Can I backtest before risking money?

Yes — the bundled Pine Script runs in TradingView's free Strategy Tester, and StockArtha shows per-symbol backtest stats plus a paper portfolio on real prices.

Is this investment advice?

No. StockArtha is analysis and automation tooling. ICT concepts have no universal definition — the exact rules are printed on every chart and in the docs.

How do I get alerts on my phone?

In-app notifications, a free Telegram bot, and a TradingView webhook endpoint. Settings → Alerts gives you the webhook URL and passphrase.

Is my account private?

Yes. Accounts are stored locally with scrypt-hashed passwords. Nothing is sold or shared.
