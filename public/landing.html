Explainable rules

Five numbered conditions. No black box.

Every signal prints exactly which rules passed or failed. No signal is ever generated from an unfinished candle.

1

Strong wick rejection

lower wick ≥ 1.5 × body, close in the top 30% of range, bullish candle, volume > 20-SMA.

2

Bullish order block

The last bearish candle before an impulse of ≥ 2 × ATR(14) within 3 bars — tracked until mitigated or expired.

3

Inducement sweep

Price sweeps the block low by ≤ 1.5 × ATR and closes back above. Deep breaks invalidate the zone.

4

4H RSI filter

Higher-timeframe RSI(14) on true 4-hour candles must be under 50 at the trigger bar. Configurable and switchable.

5

Trade management

Entry at the trigger close, stop 3%, target 10%, time exit after 25 bars. All four are configurable.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need a paid data API or broker account?

No. Live quotes and candles come from free public endpoints (Yahoo Finance plus NSE/BSE archives for the symbol masters). Dhan is optional and only used for real order execution.

Are the prices on this page real?

Yes. The chart and signal feed are fetched live from the running scanner's API. If the provider is unreachable you'll see an explicit “data unavailable” state — we never substitute random or stale prices.

Can I backtest before risking money?

Yes. The bundled Pine Script runs in TradingView's free Strategy Tester, the app shows per-symbol backtest stats on real history, and paper trading replays signals on live prices.

How do I get alerts on my phone?

In-app notifications, a free Telegram bot, and a TradingView webhook endpoint. Settings → Alerts gives you the webhook URL and passphrase to paste into a TradingView alert.

Why an “improved” preset instead of the strict ICT rules?

The literal spec (wick must sit on the sweep bar, ≤0.3×ATR sweep) fires almost never on daily large-caps. The default preset relaxes only those two constraints; the strict spec is still selectable and documented.

Is this investment advice?

No. StockArtha is analysis and automation tooling. ICT concepts have no universal definition — the exact rules are printed on every chart and in the docs.
