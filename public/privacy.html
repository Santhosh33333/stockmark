Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address (used as your login) and a password hash. There is no email OTP system.
  • Profile data — an optional display name and your preferences (theme, watchlists, risk settings).
  • Usage data — the symbols you analyse, your paper-trade history and saved settings, stored to run the product.
  • Integration credentials — if you connect Dhan, your API client id and access token are stored on the server.

How credentials are handled

Broker API secrets are stored server-side and are never sent to the browser. They are used only to fetch option-chain data or place orders that you explicitly request. You can remove them at any time in Settings.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not place trades without your explicit action and a configured broker connection.
  • We do not fabricate market prices; market data comes from the providers listed in the docs.

Cookies & sessions

We use a session cookie solely to keep you signed in. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Data retention & deletion

Your account data and paper-trade history are retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing the contact below; we will remove it within a reasonable period.

Third-party services

Market data is retrieved from Yahoo Finance, NSE and BSE public endpoints, and optionally Dhan. No emails are sent by the product. These providers process data under their own policies.

Children

TradeMind is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “last updated” date above.
